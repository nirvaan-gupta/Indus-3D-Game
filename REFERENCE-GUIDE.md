# References for a detailed reconstruction

The current campus remains approximate. Additional decorative geometry alone cannot establish the real school's minute details.

## Send these first

1. A current overhead campus screenshot or permitted site plan. Mark the administration block, academic wings, boarding, sports facilities, pool, entrances and connecting paths. Include north and a scale bar if available. A satellite screenshot is a visual reference, not an extractable 3D model.
2. Clear photos of the main entrance, administration block and both academic wings: one full front view, each side, rear and several close-ups. Name the building and viewing direction for each file.
3. At least one measured dimension per building, such as frontage width, floor height or door width. Approximate measurements should be marked as such.

## Minute details

- Windows: full facade showing their count, then close-ups of frames, mullions and recesses.
- Columns, capitals, cornices, balcony rails, roof edges and domes: close-ups plus wider context.
- Doors, steps, ramps, pavement, kerbs and level changes.
- Signs and facade colors, garden beds, trees, benches and flagpoles.
- Courtyards and corridors: a slow continuous walkthrough video with turns and building labels.
- Interiors, if wanted: permitted floor plans and room/corridor references. Only provide areas you are authorized to document; omit private offices, records and security infrastructure.

Photograph in daylight with overlapping views and minimal wide-angle distortion. Original full-resolution files are preferable. Avoid identifiable students or blur them before sending. Please do not enter restricted areas or fly a drone without the required permission.

For General Ray's character, a reference photo may be supplied if a recognizable likeness is desired. The current model is an army-inspired stylized avatar; its outfit and appearance are not verified.

Suggested naming: `administration_front_01.jpg`, `west-wing_side_02.jpg`, `campus_annotated.png`. A small batch covering one building is enough to start; the entire campus is not required at once.
